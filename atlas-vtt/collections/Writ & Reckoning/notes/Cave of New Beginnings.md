# Cave of New Beginnings

## 1 · Entrance and guardian

Exits: a narrows to the east. The cave opens to the outside to the north. Terrain: Rubble heap and Shallow water. Growing here: ferns and moss.

## 2 · Puzzle or roleplay

Exits: 2 narrows to the south and west. In the room: 3 clusters of crystals. Terrain: Rubble heap and Rimstone dams.

## 3 · Trick or setback

Exits: 2 narrows to the north and south. Terrain: Underground lake and Shallow water.

## 4 · Climax

Exits: 2 narrows to the north and east. In the room: 2 clusters of stalagmites. Terrain: Rubble heap ×2.

## 5 · Reward or revelation

Exits: a narrows to the west. Terrain: Rubble heap.
