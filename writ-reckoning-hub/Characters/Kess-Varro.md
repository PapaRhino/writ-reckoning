# Kess Varro — Rival NPC

- **Name:** Kess Varro
- **Role:** Rival — shard-hunter and rival summoner, after the same crater shard for a collector's purse
- **Home:** No fixed home; she works the west road between hamlets, hiring on where the pay is best
- **Affinity:** Reyer (creature shards), turned to profit rather than protection
- **Vessel:** A thief's cloak with shards sewn into its lining (Worn, +3 HP)
- **DEF:** 4
- **Max Health:** 11
- **Current Health:** 11
- **Health Dice:** d6
- **AP:** 6 per turn (HOUSE RULE)

## Stats and dice pools

HOUSE RULE: pool d6s succeed on 4+. Check total = successes + modifier die. Difficulties: Easy 3, Standard 5, Hard 7, Heroic 9.

- **Command:** 4 — pool 4d6, max 4. Her allies fight under her Command as their Defense (sourcebook: allies use their controller's Command as Defense).
- **Imagination:** 3 — pool 3d6, max 3
- **Might:** 2 — pool 2d6, max 2
- **Finesse:** 4 — pool 4d6, max 4. Quick, quiet, and gone before you look twice.
- **Craft:** 3 — pool 3d6, max 3. Locks, traps, and secondhand gear — all familiar tools.
- **Essence:** 2 — pool 2d6, max 2

## Abilities (4)

- **Vessel — Lined cloak:** Her Forms ride in her cloak's lining, close and quiet. She can summon without flourish and vanish without a bow.
- **Defensive style — Shadow step:** DEF 4. She dodges by being half a step ahead of the blow — and half a step toward the nearest exit.
- **Affinity — Reyer's bargain:** Creature shards answer her readily, but she treats allies as hired help: loyal while paid, gone when the purse empties.
- **Physical trait — A collector's eye:** She prices a shard at a glance, spots a forgery, and notices what a room's owner values most. It is a gift and a tell.

## Description

Kess is thirtyish, sharp-tongued, well-dressed in a road-worn way, and honest about exactly one thing: she is here for money. A collector in a distant town has offered a purse for a true crater shard, and Kess intends to collect it. She is not cruel — she will warn a party off a dangerous path, share a campfire, and even split a find — but she will not let sentiment stand between her and the purse, and she has already sold one expedition's map to a second buyer this year.

## What she wants

The same shard the party seeks, intact, and out of the crater before anyone else claims it. Failing that, a share of it, a trade, or information worth selling. She does not want the crater drained — a dead crater pays no one twice — which is why she can, under the right offer, become an ally against the villain.

## How to use her

- **In Alderford:** she arrives asking questions, sizing up the party, and offering a partnership she does not quite mean.
- **At the Watcher's Shelf:** the rivalry turns real — a race, a standoff, or a negotiation over a blocked trail. She fights to win, not to kill, and retreats if the fight turns lethal.
- **At the climax:** the GM decides by the party's treatment of her — if they dealt fairly, she arrives to harry the villain's flank; if they crossed her, she arrives to take the shard in the confusion. Either way she is a complication, not the final enemy.

## Secrets (GM only)

Kess's collector does not exist — not as she describes him. The purse is real, but the buyer is a middleman for the villain's own network, and Kess does not know it yet. If the party tells her plainly and proves it (the villain's mark on her contract, found in the grotto antechamber), she turns on the villain for real, and furiously.
