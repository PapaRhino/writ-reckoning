## Abilities (4, as per character creation)

- **Vessel — Bonded ridge-hound:** Rowan's vessel is not an object but a living bond: a ridge-hound who travels at her side and carries her Forms in the bond itself. Per the sourcebook, Bound vessels give no direct physical protection, but the hound is a real companion — it can scout, track, and warn, within common sense and GM discretion.
- **Defensive style — Dexterous Dodging:** DEF 4. She avoids harm by not being where the blow lands — quick, quiet, and hard to pin down on broken ground.
- **Affinity — Viveseviv's wild sense (elements/nature affinity):** Her affinity is to Viveseviv, who controls the distribution of elements and nature shards. She reads land, weather, and living things the way Luz reads books.
- **Physical trait — Silent step and far-seeing eyes:** She moves quietly over rough ground and spots movement at distances others miss. Years of tracking left her light-footed and sharp-eyed.

## Backstory

Rowan grew up in a hamlet on the crater's west rim, where the wilderness starts at the back fence and children learn tracking the way other children learn chores. Her family hunted and trapped the rim country, and she learned the land the slow way — by walking it in every season, with a hound at her side. The bond with her ridge-hound deepened the way such bonds do for those raised close to living things, until the hound became her vessel: not a thing she carries, but a companion she travels with.

She hires on as tracker and scout for expeditions that need to move quietly through rough country, and she knows the crater rim's trails, water, and hiding places better than anyone the party is likely to meet. She joined this expedition because the rim country she knows has started behaving like country she does not — trails gone strange, creatures restless, shards where shards should not be — and a tracker follows that kind of sign the way a hound follows scent.

## Personality + roleplaying hooks

- **Quiet, then exact.** She speaks little and observes much; when she does speak, it is usually something the party needed to hear two minutes ago. Listen the first time.
- **Land-first loyalty.** She trusts what the ground tells her over what people claim. A smooth story that contradicts fresh tracks will lose to the tracks every time.
- **Hook — the hound:** the ridge-hound is a companion, not a tool. It growls at what it distrusts and settles beside people it accepts; the GM can use its reactions as honest signals.
- **Hook — trail signs:** she leaves small trail marks for the party's way back — stacked stones, bent twigs. If the party ever needs to retreat in a hurry, those marks matter.

## Adventure role

Intro one-shot PC — the party's Finesse scout and nature reader: she tracks, sneaks, spots danger early, and reads the crater rim's wilderness. The fifth pregen — the roster works with any 4–5 players, and with five she covers the party's mobile, scouting edge.

## Continuation hook (intro one-shot — thread stays open)

The expedition's immediate job resolves in session one, but Rowan's story does not seal shut: the rim country will keep showing strange signs after this night, and the tracker who found the first sign will be the one asked to follow the next. The larger thread stays open for a second session.
