## Abilities (4, as per character creation)

- **Vessel — Crystal-embedded field journal:** Her Forms are set into a book she writes in daily — observations, sketches, and half-finished theories alongside the shards themselves. Losing the book would lose her notes and her Forms together, and she guards it accordingly.
- **Defensive style — Mental Diversion:** DEF 3. She avoids harm by misdirection — a quick spell, a thrown voice, a step that was never quite where the blow expected. She has no armor and no illusions about surviving a fair hit.
- **Affinity — Ginanig's palette (spell affinity):** Her affinity is to Ginanig, who controls the distribution of spell shards. Per the sourcebook, abstract spell Forms reward high Imagination — hers is 4 — and spells take effect immediately unless they create a lasting effect.
- **Physical trait — Sharp eyes, ink-stained hands:** She notices small things — a changed glyph, a fresh footprint, a smudged margin — and her hands are permanently ink-marked from writing. Quick fingers, careful eyes.

## Backstory

Luz studied in the scriptorium of a hillside monastery school, where she was the student who stayed late among the scrolls not from ambition but from curiosity: she wanted to know how things worked, and then she wanted to know why they worked that way instead of another. Her field journal began as a copyist's exercise and became her vessel almost without her noticing — a book she carried everywhere, whose cover crystals she polished the way other students polished their shoes. She finished her apprenticeship with good marks, ink on her hands, and no interest in a quiet scholar's post.

She came west to the crater because the crater is the largest unanswered question in Paldea, and unanswered questions are the only thing that reliably gets her out of bed before noon. She travels light, writes everything down, and has a habit of reading her notes aloud when she thinks nobody is listening. She is the party member most likely to identify what a strange shard actually is — and the least likely to survive being wrong about it in person, which is why she prefers to let Tomas stand in front.

## Personality + roleplaying hooks

- **Curious before cautious.** A strange glyph, an odd energy reading, a locked mechanism — she moves toward it. The party's job is to make sure she does so with someone standing beside her.
- **Thinks out loud.** Theories arrive half-formed and get finished in conversation. A sharp party member can catch her errors early; a careless one can get talked into anything.
- **Hook — the journal:** she offers to show pages, never to hand the book over. Anyone who reads over her shoulder will find the margins more useful than the main text.
- **Hook — tools and tinkering:** give her a broken mechanism and an hour and she will either fix it or explain, at length, why it was never going to work. Both outcomes are usually worth hearing.

## Adventure role

One-shot PC — the party's Craft/Imagination tinkerer-scholar: she identifies strange shards and mechanisms, works spell Forms, and solves problems the fighters cannot hit.

## Continuation hook (intro one-shot — thread stays open)

The expedition's immediate job resolves in session one, but Luz's story does not seal shut: her journal will hold more questions at the end of the night than at the start — above all, where the hoarded shards are coming from, and who taught the hoarder to bind them. The larger thread stays open for a second session.
