# Tamsin, Keeper of the Storehouse — Support NPC

- **Name:** Tamsin
- **Role:** Support — keeper of Alderford's village storehouse and shrine; supplies, healing, and gossip
- **Home:** Alderford, west Paldea, below the Great Crater's rim
- **Affinity:** Viveseviv (elements / nature), by village tradition
- **Vessel:** A storehouse ledger bound in oiled cloth, with a single shard pressed into its clasp (Carried)
- **DEF:** 2
- **Max Health:** 8
- **Current Health:** 8
- **Health Dice:** d6

## Stats and dice pools

HOUSE RULE: pool d6s succeed on 4+. Check total = successes + modifier die. Difficulties: Easy 3, Standard 5, Hard 7, Heroic 9.

- **Command:** 3 — pool 3d6, max 3. She runs the storehouse; villagers listen when she says what is and is not available.
- **Imagination:** 2 — pool 2d6, max 2
- **Might:** 2 — pool 2d6, max 2
- **Finesse:** 2 — pool 2d6, max 2
- **Craft:** 4 — pool 4d6, max 4. Rope, lanterns, poultices, preserved food — everything the village needs to keep going, she keeps.
- **Essence:** 3 — pool 3d6, max 3

## Abilities (4)

- **Vessel — Storehouse ledger:** Her shard is a keeper's shard: it knows what the storehouse holds and, in a pinch, what a traveler most needs from it.
- **Defensive style — Behind the counter:** DEF 2. She is no fighter; her defense is distance, a barred door, and neighbors who answer when she calls.
- **Affinity — Village providence:** She can stretch supplies further than seems possible and knows which hamlet has what Alderford lacks.
- **Physical trait — Ledger memory:** She remembers who borrowed what, who returned it late, and who never returned it at all — going back years.

## Description

Tamsin is fortyish, brisk, kind in the way that does not waste words, and the true center of Alderford. The storehouse she keeps holds the village's rope, lantern oil, dried food, spare blankets, and healing poultices, and the shrine beside it holds its prayers. Travelers who treat her fairly find their packs mysteriously fuller; travelers who steal from the storehouse find every door in the village closed to them by nightfall.

## What she wants

The village fed, the flicker understood, and the party sent up the mountain properly equipped rather than half-equipped. She will outfit the party from the storehouse (see the adventure's treasure list) and ask only that they bring back word — and, if they can, the reason the springs taste of iron.

## How to use her

- **In Alderford:** the supply stop. She is also the village gossip filter: she knows who saw lights on the rim, who sold a map, and who has been asking about shards.
- **After the climax:** she is the homecoming — a warm storehouse, a ledger entry for the party's deed, and the first person to ask what the party intends to do with the shard.
- **She does not travel.** Do not send her up the mountain; her power is the village itself, and it stays there.
