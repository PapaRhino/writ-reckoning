## Abilities (4, as per character creation)

- **Vessel — Herb-cloak:** Her Forms are sewn into a cloak she wears on every walk — herb pockets, stitched pockets for shards, a hood that smells faintly of rosemary. This vessel type determines her first Form's nature affinity.
- **Defensive style — Bramble Ward:** DEF 3. She avoids harm by stepping behind growth, shadow, and misdirection — a hedge between her and the blow, a sidestep into brush — not by armor.
- **Affinity — Viveseviv's kinship (elements / nature affinity):** Her affinity is to Viveseviv, who controls the distribution of elements and nature shards. She reads living things the way others read writing, and her nature Forms answer readily to her Essence.
- **Physical trait — Gatherer's hands:** Calloused palms, soil under the nails no matter how she scrubs, and a nose that can tell a healing herb from a harmful lookalike by smell alone. She walks softly in wild places and is noticed by wild creatures before she notices them.

## Backstory

Willa grew up in a marsh hamlet on Paldea's west road, where her mother kept the village stillroom — drying herbs, setting poultices, sitting up with the sick through long nights. Willa learned the work at her mother's elbow: which leaf staunches, which root settles a fever, which berry you never, ever pick. Her cloak came to her the way vessels do for those raised in the practice — first as a work cloak, then, stitch by stitch, as the thing that held her Forms. She has walked the west country ever since as a wandering healer, trading poultices and advice for supper and a dry loft, following the old paths between hamlets that larger folk forget exist.

She has come to the crater rim because the plants there are wrong. Herbs that should heal now wilt in the hand, spring water tastes of iron, and the wild creatures near the rim are skittish in a way she has never seen. A healer notices when the land itself is unwell, and she does not intend to stand by while it worsens. She travels with a basket, a knife, a coil of twine, and more patience than the rest of the party combined — which the party will need.

## Personality + roleplaying hooks

- **Patient, then blunt.** She will listen to a full story without interrupting, then say the one true thing nobody wanted to hear.
- **Everyone is a patient.** She checks on wounded allies, wounded strangers, and occasionally wounded enemies, in that order — and she expects the party to hold still for it.
- **Hook — the basket:** she gathers as she walks, even on a dangerous road. A strange plant near the path will slow the whole party while she examines it. It is usually worth it.
- **Hook — the cloak:** she lends her cloak to a shivering child without a second thought, but never to an adult who asks for it as a curiosity. The Forms in the hem are not ornaments.

## Adventure role

One-shot PC — the party's Essence wanderer and healer: she reads the wild, steadies the wounded, and knows when the land itself is trying to tell the party something.
