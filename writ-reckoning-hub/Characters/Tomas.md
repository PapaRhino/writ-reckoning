## Abilities (4, as per character creation)

- **Vessel — Gem-socketed war hammer:** His Forms are inlaid in a hammer he actually works with — it is a tool first and a vessel second, and he treats both jobs seriously.
- **Defensive style — Parry and footwork (Dexterous Dodging):** DEF 4. He avoids harm by moving and turning blows aside with the hammer's haft, not by standing behind armor. Quick on his feet for a big man.
- **Affinity — Consnoc's craft (equipment affinity):** His affinity is to Consnoc, who controls the distribution of equipment shards. He is the party's gear hand: summoned items arrive ready and stay useful while he maintains them (sourcebook: summoned items persist only while the controller maintains them).
- **Physical trait — Quarryman's build:** Broad shoulders, calloused hands, and a back built by years of lifting stone. He opens stuck doors the way other people knock on them.

## Backstory

Tomas grew up working the stone country around a desert-edge village of water and rock, where he learned quarrying from his father and hammer-work from anyone who would let him hold the tools. His vessel found him the way it finds most working people — not in a ceremony, but in the slow realization that the hammer he reached for every morning was also the thing that held his Forms. He never saw a reason to separate the two: a good hammer breaks rock, and a good hammer with a shard in it breaks worse things.

He came to the crater rim because the rim country always needs strong hands — trail work, hauling, clearing falls — and because the pay for guiding an expedition beats the pay for clearing a trail. He does not pretend to understand the crater's strange energy the way a shepherd reads weather, or the old books the way Luz does. He understands weight, leverage, and where to stand so the falling thing does not land on you, and in a one-night expedition that turns out to be most of what matters.

## Personality + roleplaying hooks

- **Few words, kept promises.** He says what he means the first time. If he says he will hold a position, he holds it.
- **Fixer, not talker.** Faced with a problem, his first instinct is to pick up a tool. The party will need to remind him when a problem wants words instead.
- **Hook — the hammer:** he names his tools and talks to them while working. Teasing him about it earns a grin, not a grudge — unless you touch the hammer without asking.
- **Hook — heights and edges:** quarry work taught him to respect edges. He is the party member who checks a ledge before anyone leans on it, and he will physically pull a careless ally back.

## Adventure role

One-shot PC — the party's Might/Finesse physical fighter: he hits hard, moves well, handles gear and obstacles, and holds ground while the summoners work.

## Continuation hook (intro one-shot — thread stays open)

The expedition's immediate job resolves in session one, but Tomas's story does not seal shut: the crater shard trade reaches back along the trails he works, and the folk who haul for it will come looking for the man who got in their way. The larger thread stays open for a second session.
