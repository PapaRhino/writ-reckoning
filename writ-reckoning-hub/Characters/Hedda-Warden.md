# Hedda, Warden of the Rim — Ally NPC

- **Name:** Hedda
- **Role:** Ally — retired warden of the crater-rim trails; the party's guide, shelter, and local memory
- **Home:** A stone hut on the trail above Alderford, west Paldea, below the Great Crater's rim
- **Affinity:** Viveseviv (elements / nature), by long habit rather than formal training
- **Vessel:** A walking staff with a single worn shard socket (Held)
- **DEF:** 3
- **Max Health:** 10
- **Current Health:** 10
- **Health Dice:** d6

## Stats and dice pools

HOUSE RULE: pool d6s succeed on 4+. Check total = successes + modifier die. Difficulties: Easy 3, Standard 5, Hard 7, Heroic 9.

- **Command:** 4 — pool 4d6, max 4. She has led trail crews and lost hikers alike; people listen when she points.
- **Imagination:** 2 — pool 2d6, max 2
- **Might:** 2 — pool 2d6, max 2
- **Finesse:** 3 — pool 3d6, max 3
- **Craft:** 3 — pool 3d6, max 3. Trail repair, snares, shelters — practical work, done right.
- **Essence:** 4 — pool 4d6, max 4. She reads weather, water, and animal behavior the way others read letters.

## Abilities (4)

- **Vessel — Warden's staff:** Her single shard is a trail-light: it steadies a path, marks a safe crossing, and has talked more than one lost traveler home.
- **Defensive style — Patient footing:** DEF 3. She is simply never where a falling rock, a lunging beast, or a careless blow expects her to be.
- **Affinity — Rim knowledge:** She knows the rim trails, the seasonal springs, and which slopes slide after rain. This is knowledge, not magic, and it is worth more than most spells on the mountain.
- **Physical trait — Weather-lined face, sharp eyes:** Decades outdoors. She spots a changed trail, a fresh track, or a lie about a route at a glance.

## Description

Hedda is sixty-odd, lean, grey-braided, and unimpressed by titles. She kept the rim trails for thirty years — clearing falls, marking springs, carrying out the injured — and retired to a hut that is still, somehow, the first place anyone in trouble walks to. She feeds travelers, charges nothing she can see they cannot pay, and remembers every expedition that went up the mountain and did not come back the same.

## What she wants

The crater's flicker frightens her more than she admits: the lights along the rim have dimmed, the springs run iron-tasting, and the wild creatures are restless. She wants the party to find the cause and stop it — and she wants them alive afterward. She will guide them to the rim, lend them her hut, and tell them the truth about the old grotto, including the part that makes the expedition dangerous.

## How to use her

- **In Alderford:** the party's first real conversation. She gives the hook, the warning, and the trail.
- **On the road:** she travels with the party to the Watcher's Shelf, then turns back — she will not enter the grotto, and the GM should not let her solve the climax. Her absence is the point: the last stretch is the party's alone.
- **If the party is stuck:** she can be found again at her hut with one more piece of trail wisdom. She never volunteers the grotto's secret unprompted; the party must ask the right question.

## Secrets (GM only)

Hedda entered the grotto once, decades ago, as a young warden, and left something of hers behind there — a carved trail marker, still standing. It is the one landmark the villain has not disturbed, because he does not know what it means. A party that asks her about her own first trip learns where it is, and it becomes their safest approach to the climax.
