## Abilities (4, as per character creation)

- **Vessel — Shard-laden mantle:** His Forms are set into a mantle weighed down with crater shards — more shards than any one summoner should carry, and the reason the crater country around him behaves strangely.
- **Defensive style — Armored Bulwark (mantle-warded):** DEF 5. He meets harm behind the warded weight of his mantle and does not move for it. As with armored styles, he is sturdy in a fight and poor at moving quietly.
- **Affinity — Viveseviv's hoard (elements/nature affinity, abused):** His affinity is to Viveseviv, but where a natural summoner works with living forces, Severo stockpiles them. His power is broad and deep — Essence 5 — and it is the reason local water, weather, and creatures have grown restless.
- **Physical trait — Shard-touched hands and unblinking stare:** His hands are marked from handling raw shards, and he holds eye contact a beat too long. People who meet him remember the stare before they remember his words.

## Description

Severo was once a rim-country scholar of natural forces — the kind of man villages asked about strange weather and stranger water. Somewhere along the way, study became collection, and collection became hoarding: he now strips crater shards from the land around the Great Crater and binds them into his mantle, heedless of what the taking does to the country. He is not raving; he is calm, certain, and convinced the shards are safer — and better used — in his keeping than in the ground. That certainty is what makes him dangerous: he will explain himself politely while his creatures do the violence.

## GM note — how to run him

- **Goals:** Hold the shard cache and keep collecting; prove — to the party, to the rim villages, to himself — that his keeping is stewardship, not theft; do not be stopped by "sentimental" arguments about land and water.
- **Tactics:** He opens with his strongest ally and holds the center of the fight (see `Cards/Severo-Forms.md`), spending his full AP on high-tier summons while weaker allies screen him. He talks during combat — offers, justifications, bargains — and if reduced to low Health he bargains in earnest: information, a shard, or a truce in exchange for his life. He does not fight to the death for pride; he fights to the death only if the party leaves him no road out.
- **What he knows:** Where the shards come from and how they are bound; that the taking is what soured the rim's water and stirred its creatures (he considers this a price, not a problem); and the name or mark of whoever buys shards from collectors like Vicente — the thread that continues past this session.
- **How to run the confrontation:** Give the party a real choice, not just a fight: talk him down, trade, drive him off, or defeat him. Any outcome should leave the cache's fate and the buyer's identity as open threads. If he escapes, he escapes with fewer shards and a grudge — ideal for a return.

## Adventure role

Villain NPC for the intro one-shot — the antagonist abusing Forms and crater shards; the source of the session's immediate problem, but not the end of the larger thread.

## Continuation hook (intro one-shot — thread stays open, by design)

Severo is the first session's antagonist, NOT a series finale. Whether he is defeated, driven off, or bargained with, the larger thread stays open: the shard buyers beyond the rim, the question of who taught him to bind shards this way, and the land's slow recovery all continue into later sessions. Do not write him a sealed ending.
