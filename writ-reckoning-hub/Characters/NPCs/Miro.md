## Abilities (4, as per character creation)

- **Vessel — Tool satchel:** His Forms ride among the tools of his trade — a provisioner who mends, fits, and supplies, and whose vessel looks exactly like his workbag because it is one.
- **Defensive style — Unarmored (shopkeeper's dodge):** DEF 2. He avoids harm by stepping back, ducking behind a counter, or simply not being where trouble is. He is no fighter and the numbers say so.
- **Affinity — Consnoc's fitting hand (equipment affinity):** His affinity is to Consnoc. He knows gear — what fits, what holds, what will break under load — and he can judge a stranger's equipment at a glance.
- **Physical trait — Craftsman's hands and a merchant's memory:** Thick-knuckled hands from a lifetime of mending, and a memory for faces, debts, and who bought what, when, that younger folk find unnerving.

## Description

Miró runs the provisions store in a hamlet on the crater's west rim — rope, rations, tools, lamp oil, and whatever the rim country needs this week — and serves as one of the hamlet's elders when disputes need settling. He has sold supplies to every expedition that ever passed through, including Severo's people and Vicente, and he remembers all of them. He is friendly, fair, and impossible to hurry.

## GM note — how to run him

- **Goals:** Keep his hamlet supplied and safe; keep trade moving; know who passes through and why.
- **Tactics:** He does not fight. In danger he withdraws, bars the door, and waits. His power at the table is information and supplies, not combat.
- **What he knows:** Who bought what before heading to the crater — Severo's people bought rope, digging tools, and shard-carrying cases in quantity; Vicente bought light, fast gear and paid in coin, not trade. He knows the rim trails' gossip: which paths went strange, whose animals grew restless, and that the well-water changed taste around the time the heavy buying started. He does NOT know the cache's location — but he knows who might: Rosa, and the trail signs Rowan can read.
- **How he helps without stealing the scene:** He sells, mends, and tells. A party that treats him fairly gets fair prices, honest warnings, and one good rumor. A party that threatens him gets polite silence and the worst rope in the store.

## Adventure role

Supporting NPC for the intro one-shot — the crater-rim hamlet's provisioner and elder; the party's supply stop, rumor source, and first hint that the shard collecting is organized.

## Continuation hook (intro one-shot — thread stays open)

Miró stays in the story after the first session: his store ledger is a record of who has been buying shard gear, and future sessions can return to it — and to him — whenever the party wants to trace the buyers.
