## Abilities (4, as per character creation)

- **Vessel — Herb pouch:** Her Forms ride among dried herbs and gathered stones — a healer's vessel, carried everywhere, smelling faintly of the hills.
- **Defensive style — Dexterous Dodging (trail-wise):** DEF 3. She avoids harm by knowing the ground — where to step, when to duck, when to simply not be on the trail anymore.
- **Affinity — Viveseviv's green hand (elements/nature affinity):** Her affinity is to Viveseviv. She knows plants, water, and weather, and her healing — magical or otherwise — draws on that connection.
- **Physical trait — Surefooted and weather-worn:** Years on rim trails left her steady on broken ground and unbothered by weather that sends younger guides back indoors.

## Description

Rosa is a guide and healer from a hamlet on the crater's west rim, middle-aged, practical, and famously hard to rattle. Travelers hire her because she knows the trails and because, when someone gets hurt on them, she is the person already kneeling beside them with the pouch open. She speaks plainly, charges fairly, and does not repeat gossip — which is why half the rim country tells her things anyway.

## GM note — how to run her

- **Goals:** Get travelers through the rim country alive; keep the crater's strangeness from hurting ordinary folk; protect her hamlet's water and grazing.
- **Tactics:** She never leads a fight. In combat she hangs back, keeps allies on their feet (non-magical healing is a Craft check per the sourcebook's skill table; magical healing is Imagination), and points out terrain — cover, water, high ground — the party might miss.
- **What she knows:** The rim trails and their recent changes (which paths have gone strange, where creatures have grown restless); that someone has been collecting crater shards in quantity; and that the hamlet's well-water has tasted faintly wrong since the collecting began. She does NOT know the villain's name or lair — she can point the party toward the signs, not the door.
- **How she helps without stealing the scene:** She answers questions, patches wounds between fights, and warns once — clearly — before danger. She does not solve puzzles, win fights, or make the party's decisions.

## Adventure role

Ally NPC for the intro one-shot — the party's guide and healer on the crater rim; a friendly face who knows the land and can keep the party moving. She is not a fifth player character and should not be run as one.

## Continuation hook (intro one-shot — thread stays open)

Rosa's hamlet and its water remain at the heart of the larger thread: whatever is wrong near the crater will still be wrong after the first session's job resolves, and she will still be the person the party calls on when it gets worse.
