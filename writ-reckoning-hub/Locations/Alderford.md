# Alderford

A farming hamlet on the west road of Paldea, below the Great Crater's rim — the starting village of [[The-Crater-Call]].

## Description

Alderford is small enough that a stranger is news by supper and a friend by breakfast. Thatch roofs, a stone well in the square, a storehouse with an oven, a shrine beside it, and fields running out toward the pine woods and the mountain. The road west from here climbs to the rim trail; the road east runs back toward the wider country. Nothing in Alderford is grand, and everything in it works — which is why the iron taste in the well water frightens people more than any monster would. When the ordinary things go wrong, the village knows something larger is wrong too.

The village lives by farming, herding, and the travelers who pass through on their way to the rim. It has no wall, no garrison, and no patience for trouble brought to its door — debts are settled on the road, not in the storehouse.

## What's here

- **The stone well** — the first place the iron taste shows. Tasting it is the party's first clue, before anyone says a word about the crater.
- **The storehouse and shrine** — Tamsin's domain: rope, lantern oil, dried food, blankets, healing poultices, and the village's gossip, all available to travelers who deal fairly.
- **Hedda's hut** — one warm room full of hide-and-bark trail maps, at the village's west edge. The expedition's briefing happens here, and the hut stays a safe place to return to.
- **The west road** — the trailhead for the rim trail. Hedda walks the party to its foot in Scene 1; after that, the road is theirs.
