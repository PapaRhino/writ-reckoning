# The Watcher's Shelf

A shelf of pale rock high on the Great Crater's rim, west Paldea, holding a ruined watchtower and a camp — the midpoint of [[The-Crater-Call]].

## Description

The Shelf is where the pine woods give way to bare rock and the crater's bright rim ring becomes something you can almost touch. The Watcher's tower stands roofless here, its stair gone to rubble, its beacon dish rusted open to the sky — a place built to watch the crater, now watching it dim. Below the tower, a spring that should run clear runs rust-red while the drain holds. The lee of the tower is the best camp on the mountain: sheltered from wind, visible from the trail, and (once the crag hounds are dealt with) safe enough for a long rest.

From the Shelf, the trail splits: the main passage down to the Hollow Heart grotto, and — marked on Hedda's map and by Kess's chalk, if the party earned it — the narrow chimney entrance above the main passage, fit for small allies and nimble climbers.

## What's here

- **The ruined tower** — high ground, a landmark, and the Scene 3 fight's Center of Combat (the fallen lintel). Climbing what remains of it gives a view of the whole rim and the grotto mouth below.
- **The spring** — the Scene 2 fight's objective. Its rust-red water is the adventure's clearest evidence that the crater is being drained, not poisoned.
- **The loose scree slope** — the Scene 2 exploration obstacle. Crossed carefully, with rope, or by the goat path a good tracker finds.
- **The Shelf camp** — the adventure's one safe long rest (8 hours, roll 2 Health Dice). Kess may leave a note and a flask of lantern oil here if the party dealt with her in Alderford.
