# The Hollow Heart Grotto

A crystal grotto beneath the Great Crater's rim, west Paldea — the climax site of [[The-Crater-Call]].

## Description

The Hollow Heart is not dark, and that is the first wrong thing about it. It is lit the way a room is lit when someone has gathered every lamp in a village into one place — light pooled in the center, around a rig of iron and chalk, with three dark lines running from a cracked lodestone out to the walls and down into the mountain, drinking. The air hums. The light is thin at the edges, as if it is being pulled somewhere it does not want to go. Somewhere below, past the rig, is the crater floor and its lake — the place the shard belongs, if the party chooses to return it.

The grotto has two entrances: the main passage from the Shelf trail, facing Corvin's wards, and a narrow chimney above it, fit for small allies and nimble climbers, opening behind the rig. The antechamber before the main chamber holds the warden's marker and the scorched first circle — the two findings that change the climax fight from a brawl into a plan.

## What's here

- **The antechamber** — the warden's carved trail marker (Hedda's, from decades ago; the safest approach to the rig) and the scorched first circle (proof the lodestone was cracked deliberately, not by accident — the fact that makes Corvin falter for one turn if spoken aloud).
- **The lodestone rig** — the climax fight's starting Center of Combat. Three siphon lines run from it; disrupting all three (1 AP interaction + a Standard Craft or Imagination check per line) breaks the siphon, removes Corvin's +1 spell damage, drops his shade's damage, and brightens the grotto.
- **The warded circle** — Corvin's defensive rings of chalk, iron, and old spell-work. He is weakest when drawn out of it; the party's best tactic is to make him leave it, not to fight him inside it.
- **The shard's cradle** — where the crater shard rests once the siphon breaks: a fist-sized crystal, warm and bright. Taking it makes the carrier the Center of Combat. What the party does with it is the adventure's ending, and it is theirs to choose.
- **Corvin's journals** — his scholar's notes on the siphon, the first circle, and the buyer's network. The paper trail for any continuation session.
