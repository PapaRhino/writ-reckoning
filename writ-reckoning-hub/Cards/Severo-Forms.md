# Severo Vane — Forms

Character: `Characters/NPCs/Severo.md`. Card Pool 4 — 4 Forms listed, within pool.

## Conventions used here

- **Conversion (sourcebook):** Pokémon TCG numbers operate in the tens, so divide by 10 consistently for ALL cards (60 HP → 6 HP, 30 damage → 3 damage).
- **Tier bands as written (sourcebook):** Tier 1 usually ≤3 damage and ≤5 health; Tier 2 around 4–6 damage and <7 health; Tier 5 has no limits. Cards between Tier 2 and Tier 5 are placed by nearest band at GM discretion — each entry says how.
- **Allies (sourcebook):** allies have HP and Damage but no set Defense — they use their controller's Command (Severo: 4) as Defense, and vanish at 0 HP.
- **AP costs (HOUSE RULE):** 6 AP per turn, refreshing at turn start. Summon Tier 1 = 2 AP, Tier 2 = 3 AP, Tier 3 = 4 AP, Tier 4 = 5 AP, Tier 5 = 6 AP; move near = 1 AP, far = 2 AP; attack with ally/item = 2 AP; simple interaction = 1 AP; maintain a lasting effect = 1 AP/turn.

## 1. Haunter (Creature)

- **Card:** Haunter (Pokémon TCG, classic print).
- **Type:** Creature/Ally.
- **Printed HP/damage:** 50 HP; Dream Eater 30 damage.
- **WR-converted values:** 5 HP, 3 damage (÷10).
- **Tier:** Tier 1 — damage 3 and health 5 are both within Tier 1's caps.
- **How it plays at the table:** Summon 2 AP (HOUSE RULE). Severo's watcher and whisperer: it spies, drifts through gaps, and strikes sleeping or distracted targets for 3 damage (2 AP per attack); defends with Severo's Command (4), vanishes at 0 HP. The ally the party meets before they meet Severo.

## 2. Magneton (Creature)

- **Card:** Magneton (Pokémon TCG, classic print).
- **Type:** Creature/Ally.
- **Printed HP/damage:** 80 HP; Selfdestruct 80 damage.
- **WR-converted values:** 8 HP, 8 damage (÷10).
- **Tier:** Tier 3 (GM discretion) — both values sit one step above Tier 2's bands (health 8 past the <7 cap, damage 8 past the 4–6 range) and well below the unbounded Tier 5, so it is placed at the intermediate band.
- **How it plays at the table:** Summon 4 AP (HOUSE RULE). A floating shard-magnet horror that embodies Severo's hoard. Its Selfdestruct is its signature: per the card's own text it deals its damage and then dismisses itself — GM ruling: an 8-damage burst (2 AP attack) after which Magneton vanishes, win or lose. Use it once, dramatically, not as a routine attack.

## 3. Gyarados (Creature)

- **Card:** Gyarados (Pokémon TCG, classic print).
- **Type:** Creature/Ally.
- **Printed HP/damage:** 100 HP; Dragon Rage 50 damage.
- **WR-converted values:** 10 HP, 5 damage (÷10).
- **Tier:** Tier 4 (GM discretion) — health 10 is a full step above the Tier 3 neighborhood (7–9) while damage 5 alone would be Tier 2; the combination plays as a heavyweight, below only the unbounded Tier 5.
- **How it plays at the table:** Summon 5 AP (HOUSE RULE) — nearly Severo's whole turn. A 10-HP, 5-damage terror that holds the center of the fight; attacks for 2 AP, defends with Severo's Command (4), vanishes at 0 HP. If it appears, the fight has become serious.

## 4. Charizard (Creature)

- **Card:** Charizard (Pokémon TCG, classic print).
- **Type:** Creature/Ally.
- **Printed HP/damage:** 120 HP; Fire Spin 100 damage.
- **WR-converted values:** 12 HP, 10 damage (÷10).
- **Tier:** Tier 5 — damage 10 and health 12 are far beyond Tier 2's bands, and Tier 5 is the sourcebook's tier with no limits. This is the card the tier system exists to gate.
- **How it plays at the table:** Summon 6 AP (HOUSE RULE) — Severo's entire turn, which is the point: the turn he summons it, he does nothing else. It then attacks for 2 AP per turn at 10 damage, defends with Severo's Command (4), and vanishes at 0 HP. GM advice: telegraph it (the mantle flares, the air heats) and use it as the confrontation's climax, not its opening — or as the reason the party chooses to bargain instead of fighting.
