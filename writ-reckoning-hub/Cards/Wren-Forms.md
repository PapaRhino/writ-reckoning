# Wren Ashdown — Forms

Character: `Characters/Wren.md`. Card Pool 4 — 4 Forms listed, within pool.

## Conventions used here

- **Conversion (sourcebook):** Pokémon TCG numbers operate in the tens, so divide by 10 consistently for ALL cards (60 HP → 6 HP, 30 damage → 3 damage).
- **Tier bands as written (sourcebook):** Tier 1 usually ≤3 damage and ≤5 health; Tier 2 around 4–6 damage and <7 health; Tier 5 has no limits. The sourcebook says "usually" and "so on and so forth," so cards between Tier 2 and Tier 5 are placed by nearest band at GM discretion — each entry says how.
- **Allies (sourcebook):** allies have HP and Damage but no set Defense — they use their controller's Command (Wren: 3) as Defense, and vanish at 0 HP. Attacking with an ally costs AP (below).
- **AP costs (HOUSE RULE):** 6 AP per turn, refreshing at turn start. Summon Tier 1 = 2 AP, Tier 2 = 3 AP, Tier 3 = 4 AP, Tier 4 = 5 AP, Tier 5 = 6 AP; move near = 1 AP, far = 2 AP; attack with ally/item = 2 AP; simple interaction = 1 AP; maintain a lasting effect = 1 AP/turn.

## 1. Unstable Form — Verdant's basic energy deck (Essence)

- **Card:** a deck of basic Pokémon Energy cards (Fire, Water, Grass, Lightning, Psychic, Fighting, Darkness, Metal, Fairy as printed — the deck, not one card).
- **Type:** Essence (Unstable Form).
- **Printed HP/damage:** none — energy cards carry no HP or damage.
- **WR-converted values:** none.
- **Tier:** not tiered like a creature — it is an Unstable Form, per the sourcebook.
- **Rules (sourcebook):** takes 1 card slot; usually cannot be traded away or given up without consequence; using it requires a special relationship with a Lesser Power (here Verdant, the Energy Shepherd); the deck can be upgraded (3 Soulrocks × Max Card Tier).
- **How it plays at the table:** Wren draws energy to feed her allies' and companions' Forms — the flexible energy source the party builds around. Drawing and assigning energy is a simple interaction (1 AP, HOUSE RULE) unless the GM rules a particular draw is a summon in its own right. It never attacks on its own and has no HP to lose.

## 2. Charmander (Creature)

- **Card:** Charmander (Pokémon TCG, classic print).
- **Type:** Creature/Ally.
- **Printed HP/damage:** 50 HP; Ember 30 damage.
- **WR-converted values:** 5 HP, 3 damage (÷10).
- **Tier:** Tier 1 — damage 3 is within Tier 1's ≤3 cap and health 5 is within Tier 1's ≤5 cap.
- **How it plays at the table:** Summon 2 AP (HOUSE RULE). A small, scrappy fire ally: attacks for 2 AP using Wren's Command for the attack roll, defends with Wren's Command (3) as its Defense, vanishes at 0 HP.

## 3. Eevee (Creature)

- **Card:** Eevee (Pokémon TCG, classic print).
- **Type:** Creature/Ally.
- **Printed HP/damage:** 50 HP; Quick Attack 10 damage.
- **WR-converted values:** 5 HP, 1 damage (÷10).
- **Tier:** Tier 1 — damage 1 and health 5 are both within Tier 1's caps.
- **How it plays at the table:** Summon 2 AP (HOUSE RULE). A quick, adaptable little ally — best used for harrying, carrying, and non-combat help (opening, fetching, distracting) as much as for its 1 damage; defends with Wren's Command (3), vanishes at 0 HP.

## 4. Pikachu (Creature)

- **Card:** Pikachu (Pokémon TCG, classic print).
- **Type:** Creature/Ally.
- **Printed HP/damage:** 60 HP; Thunder Jolt 30 damage.
- **WR-converted values:** 6 HP, 3 damage (÷10).
- **Tier:** Tier 2 — damage 3 fits Tier 1, but health 6 exceeds Tier 1's ≤5 health cap while staying under Tier 2's <7 health cap, so it sits one band up.
- **How it plays at the table:** Summon 3 AP (HOUSE RULE). Wren's sturdiest regular ally and her main lightning hitter: attacks for 2 AP, defends with Wren's Command (3), vanishes at 0 HP.
