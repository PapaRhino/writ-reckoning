# Vicente Corso — Forms

Character: `Characters/NPCs/Vicente.md`. Card Pool 4 — 4 Forms listed, within pool.

## Conventions used here

- **Conversion (sourcebook):** Pokémon TCG numbers operate in the tens, so divide by 10 consistently for ALL cards (60 HP → 6 HP, 30 damage → 3 damage).
- **Tier bands as written (sourcebook):** Tier 1 usually ≤3 damage and ≤5 health; Tier 2 around 4–6 damage and <7 health; Tier 5 has no limits. Cards between Tier 2 and Tier 5 are placed by nearest band at GM discretion — each entry says how.
- **Allies (sourcebook):** allies have HP and Damage but no set Defense — they use their controller's Command (Vicente: 4) as Defense, and vanish at 0 HP.
- **Spells (sourcebook):** spells take effect immediately unless they create a lasting effect.
- **AP costs (HOUSE RULE):** 6 AP per turn, refreshing at turn start. Summon Tier 1 = 2 AP, Tier 2 = 3 AP, Tier 3 = 4 AP, Tier 4 = 5 AP, Tier 5 = 6 AP; move near = 1 AP, far = 2 AP; attack with ally/item = 2 AP; simple interaction = 1 AP; maintain a lasting effect = 1 AP/turn.

## 1. Squirtle (Creature)

- **Card:** Squirtle (Pokémon TCG, classic print).
- **Type:** Creature/Ally.
- **Printed HP/damage:** 40 HP; Bubble 10 damage.
- **WR-converted values:** 4 HP, 1 damage (÷10).
- **Tier:** Tier 1 — damage 1 and health 4 are both within Tier 1's caps.
- **How it plays at the table:** Summon 2 AP (HOUSE RULE). Vicente's light screen and water hand: it holds a position, wets ground, and nips for 1 damage while his bigger allies work; defends with Vicente's Command (4), vanishes at 0 HP.

## 2. Graveler (Creature)

- **Card:** Graveler (Pokémon TCG, classic print).
- **Type:** Creature/Ally.
- **Printed HP/damage:** 60 HP; Rock Throw 30 damage.
- **WR-converted values:** 6 HP, 3 damage (÷10).
- **Tier:** Tier 2 — damage 3 fits Tier 1, but health 6 exceeds Tier 1's ≤5 health cap while staying under Tier 2's <7 cap, so it sits one band up.
- **How it plays at the table:** Summon 3 AP (HOUSE RULE). Vicente's brawler: 3 damage per attack (2 AP), defends with Vicente's Command (4), vanishes at 0 HP. The ally he sends when a race turns into a scuffle.

## 3. Electabuzz (Creature)

- **Card:** Electabuzz (Pokémon TCG, classic print).
- **Type:** Creature/Ally.
- **Printed HP/damage:** 70 HP; Thunderpunch 30 damage.
- **WR-converted values:** 7 HP, 3 damage (÷10).
- **Tier:** Tier 3 (GM discretion) — health 7 reaches the Tier 2 <7 health cap and stops there, so a 7-HP striker plays a step above Tier 2 even though its damage (3) is modest.
- **How it plays at the table:** Summon 4 AP (HOUSE RULE). Vicente's heavy: 3 damage per attack (2 AP) with 7 HP behind it, defending with Vicente's Command (4). He leads with this when he wants the party to think twice about pressing him.

## 4. Switch (Spell)

- **Card:** Switch (Pokémon TCG Trainer card, classic print).
- **Type:** Spell.
- **Printed effect:** Switch 1 of your Active Pokémon with 1 of your Benched Pokémon.
- **WR-converted values:** none — a positioning effect, not a number.
- **Tier:** Tier 1 (GM ruling) — a pure reposition with no damage or healing, comparable to a Tier 1 effect.
- **How it plays at the table:** Cast 2 AP (HOUSE RULE, Tier 1 cost). Swaps which of Vicente's summoned allies is forward without paying that ally's move cost — the relic-hunter's escape-and-replace trick. Takes effect immediately (sourcebook).
