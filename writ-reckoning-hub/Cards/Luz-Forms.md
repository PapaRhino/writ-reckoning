# Luz Ortega — Forms

Character: `Characters/Luz.md`. Card Pool 4 — 4 Forms listed, within pool.

## Conventions used here

- **Conversion (sourcebook):** Pokémon TCG numbers operate in the tens, so divide by 10 consistently for ALL cards (60 HP → 6 HP, 30 damage → 3 damage).
- **Tier bands as written (sourcebook):** Tier 1 usually ≤3 damage and ≤5 health; Tier 2 around 4–6 damage and <7 health; Tier 5 has no limits. Non-damaging spell effects are ruled by the GM on power (sourcebook).
- **Allies (sourcebook):** allies have HP and Damage but no set Defense — they use their controller's Command (Luz: 2) as Defense, and vanish at 0 HP.
- **Spells (sourcebook):** spells take effect immediately unless they create a lasting effect.
- **AP costs (HOUSE RULE):** 6 AP per turn, refreshing at turn start. Summon Tier 1 = 2 AP, Tier 2 = 3 AP, Tier 3 = 4 AP, Tier 4 = 5 AP, Tier 5 = 6 AP; move near = 1 AP, far = 2 AP; attack with ally/item = 2 AP; simple interaction = 1 AP; maintain a lasting effect = 1 AP/turn.

## 1. Abra (Creature)

- **Card:** Abra (Pokémon TCG, classic print).
- **Type:** Creature/Ally.
- **Printed HP/damage:** 30 HP; Psyshock 10 damage.
- **WR-converted values:** 3 HP, 1 damage (÷10).
- **Tier:** Tier 1 — damage 1 and health 3 are both within Tier 1's caps.
- **How it plays at the table:** Summon 2 AP (HOUSE RULE). A fragile psychic helper: useful for sensing, fetching small things, and misdirection (fitting Luz's Mental Diversion style) more than for its 1 damage; defends with Luz's Command (2), vanishes at 0 HP — keep it out of reach.

## 2. Gastly (Creature)

- **Card:** Gastly (Pokémon TCG, classic print).
- **Type:** Creature/Ally.
- **Printed HP/damage:** 30 HP; Lick 10 damage.
- **WR-converted values:** 3 HP, 1 damage (÷10).
- **Tier:** Tier 1 — damage 1 and health 3 are both within Tier 1's caps.
- **How it plays at the table:** Summon 2 AP (HOUSE RULE). A drifting, unsettling scout: it can pass where solid allies cannot (within common sense and GM discretion), harry foes for 1 damage, and — fitting a gas-like ally — slip through gaps; defends with Luz's Command (2), vanishes at 0 HP.

## 3. Bill (Spell)

- **Card:** Bill (Pokémon TCG Trainer card, classic print).
- **Type:** Spell.
- **Printed effect:** Draw 2 cards.
- **WR-converted values:** Gain 2 AP (sourcebook's Interpreting Card Effects table: "Draw a Card" usually means gain 1 AP, so Draw 2 = gain 2 AP).
- **Tier:** Tier 1 (GM ruling) — a modest resource effect with no HP or damage to band.
- **How it plays at the table:** Cast 2 AP (HOUSE RULE, Tier 1 cost), then immediately gain 2 AP back — net 0 AP, but the gained AP can be spent on other actions this turn. Spells take effect immediately (sourcebook). The scholar's tempo trick: it turns a quiet moment into one more move, attack, or maintained effect.

## 4. Super Potion (Spell)

- **Card:** Super Potion (Pokémon TCG Trainer card, classic print).
- **Type:** Spell.
- **Printed effect:** Heal 40 damage from 1 of your Pokémon.
- **WR-converted values:** Heal 4 HP (÷10).
- **Tier:** Tier 2 (GM ruling) — a heal of 4 is a clear step above Potion's Tier 1 heal of 2, so it sits one band up; it has no HP or damage to band directly.
- **How it plays at the table:** Cast 3 AP (HOUSE RULE, Tier 2 cost). Heals 4 HP on one ally or character in range; takes effect immediately (sourcebook). Luz's big patch — best saved for an ally about to vanish or a character near 0 HP.
