# Tomas Vidal — Forms

Character: `Characters/Tomas.md`. Card Pool 4 — 4 Forms listed, within pool.

## Conventions used here

- **Conversion (sourcebook):** Pokémon TCG numbers operate in the tens, so divide by 10 consistently for ALL cards (60 HP → 6 HP, 30 damage → 3 damage).
- **Tier bands as written (sourcebook):** Tier 1 usually ≤3 damage and ≤5 health; Tier 2 around 4–6 damage and <7 health; Tier 5 has no limits. Equipment is judged like creatures (sourcebook): by its numerically definable effect.
- **Allies (sourcebook):** allies have HP and Damage but no set Defense — they use their controller's Command (Tomas: 2) as Defense, and vanish at 0 HP.
- **Items (sourcebook):** summoned items persist only while the controller maintains them; if destroyed they are simply dismissed, never permanently destroyed.
- **AP costs (HOUSE RULE):** 6 AP per turn, refreshing at turn start. Summon Tier 1 = 2 AP, Tier 2 = 3 AP, Tier 3 = 4 AP, Tier 4 = 5 AP, Tier 5 = 6 AP; move near = 1 AP, far = 2 AP; attack with ally/item = 2 AP; simple interaction = 1 AP; maintain a lasting effect = 1 AP/turn.

## 1. Machop (Creature)

- **Card:** Machop (Pokémon TCG, classic print).
- **Type:** Creature/Ally.
- **Printed HP/damage:** 50 HP; Low Kick 20 damage.
- **WR-converted values:** 5 HP, 2 damage (÷10).
- **Tier:** Tier 1 — damage 2 and health 5 are both within Tier 1's caps.
- **How it plays at the table:** Summon 2 AP (HOUSE RULE). A brawling workmate for Tomas: attacks for 2 AP, defends with Tomas's Command (2) — notably soft for a front-line ally, so Tomas himself should hold the front while Machop flanks — vanishes at 0 HP.

## 2. Geodude (Creature)

- **Card:** Geodude (Pokémon TCG, classic print).
- **Type:** Creature/Ally.
- **Printed HP/damage:** 50 HP; Stone Barrage 10 damage.
- **WR-converted values:** 5 HP, 1 damage (÷10).
- **Tier:** Tier 1 — damage 1 and health 5 are both within Tier 1's caps.
- **How it plays at the table:** Summon 2 AP (HOUSE RULE). A living lump of quarry stone: slow, stubborn, and handy for blocking paths, holding doors, and soaking a hit; attacks for 2 AP, defends with Tomas's Command (2), vanishes at 0 HP.

## 3. PlusPower (Equipment)

- **Card:** PlusPower (Pokémon TCG Trainer card, classic print).
- **Type:** Equipment.
- **Printed effect:** Attach to 1 of your Pokémon; its attacks do 10 more damage.
- **WR-converted values:** +1 damage (÷10).
- **Tier:** Tier 1 (GM ruling) — a small single-target buff comparable to a Tier 1 effect; the sourcebook lets players apply offensive buffs as +1 to the attack roll OR +1 to damage.
- **How it plays at the table:** Summon 2 AP (HOUSE RULE) onto Tomas or an ally (summoning directly onto an ally equips it immediately, sourcebook). Maintaining the effect costs 1 AP/turn (HOUSE RULE). Fades if the bearer vanishes or control is lost.

## 4. Defender (Equipment)

- **Card:** Defender (Pokémon TCG Trainer card, classic print).
- **Type:** Equipment.
- **Printed effect:** Attach to 1 of your Pokémon; reduce damage from the next attack against it by 20.
- **WR-converted values:** Reduce the next attack's damage by 2 (÷10).
- **Tier:** Tier 1 (GM ruling) — a small single-use defensive effect comparable to a Tier 1 effect; the sourcebook lets defensive buffs be +1 DEF, damage reduction, or temporary HP.
- **How it plays at the table:** Summon 2 AP (HOUSE RULE) onto Tomas or an ally. Absorbs 2 damage from the next attack against the bearer, then is spent. Maintaining an unspent Defender across turns costs 1 AP/turn (HOUSE RULE).
