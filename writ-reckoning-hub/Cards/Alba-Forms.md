# Alba Ferrer — Forms

Character: `Characters/Alba.md`. Card Pool 4 — 4 Forms listed, within pool.

## Conventions used here

- **Conversion (sourcebook):** Pokémon TCG numbers operate in the tens, so divide by 10 consistently for ALL cards (60 HP → 6 HP, 30 damage → 3 damage).
- **Tier bands as written (sourcebook):** Tier 1 usually ≤3 damage and ≤5 health; Tier 2 around 4–6 damage and <7 health; Tier 5 has no limits. Cards between Tier 2 and Tier 5 are placed by nearest band at GM discretion — each entry says how.
- **Allies (sourcebook):** allies have HP and Damage but no set Defense — they use their controller's Command (Alba: 5) as Defense, and vanish at 0 HP.
- **AP costs (HOUSE RULE):** 6 AP per turn, refreshing at turn start. Summon Tier 1 = 2 AP, Tier 2 = 3 AP, Tier 3 = 4 AP, Tier 4 = 5 AP, Tier 5 = 6 AP; move near = 1 AP, far = 2 AP; attack with ally/item = 2 AP; simple interaction = 1 AP; maintain a lasting effect = 1 AP/turn.

## 1. Growlithe (Creature)

- **Card:** Growlithe (Pokémon TCG, classic print).
- **Type:** Creature/Ally.
- **Printed HP/damage:** 50 HP; Flare 20 damage.
- **WR-converted values:** 5 HP, 2 damage (÷10).
- **Tier:** Tier 1 — damage 2 and health 5 are both within Tier 1's caps.
- **How it plays at the table:** Summon 2 AP (HOUSE RULE). A loyal front-line hound: attacks for 2 AP using Alba's Command for the attack roll, defends with Alba's Command (5) — the toughest Defense any party ally carries — and vanishes at 0 HP.

## 2. Kadabra (Creature)

- **Card:** Kadabra (Pokémon TCG, classic print).
- **Type:** Creature/Ally.
- **Printed HP/damage:** 60 HP; Super Psy 50 damage.
- **WR-converted values:** 6 HP, 5 damage (÷10).
- **Tier:** Tier 2 — damage 5 sits in Tier 2's 4–6 range and health 6 sits under Tier 2's <7 cap.
- **How it plays at the table:** Summon 3 AP (HOUSE RULE). Alba's heavy hitter: 5 damage per attack (2 AP), defends with Alba's Command (5), vanishes at 0 HP. Best held slightly back and sent at priority targets on her call.

## 3. Pidgeotto (Creature)

- **Card:** Pidgeotto (Pokémon TCG, classic print).
- **Type:** Creature/Ally.
- **Printed HP/damage:** 60 HP; Mirror Move 20 damage.
- **WR-converted values:** 6 HP, 2 damage (÷10).
- **Tier:** Tier 2 — damage 2 fits Tier 1, but health 6 exceeds Tier 1's ≤5 health cap while staying under Tier 2's <7 cap, so it sits one band up.
- **How it plays at the table:** Summon 3 AP (HOUSE RULE). A flying scout and harrier: its value is reach and eyes — scouting, distracting, and hitting targets behind cover — as much as its 2 damage; defends with Alba's Command (5), vanishes at 0 HP.

## 4. Potion (Spell)

- **Card:** Potion (Pokémon TCG Trainer card, classic print).
- **Type:** Spell.
- **Printed effect:** Heal 20 damage from 1 of your Pokémon.
- **WR-converted values:** Heal 2 HP (÷10).
- **Tier:** Tier 1 (GM ruling) — a small, single-target heal comparable to a Tier 1 effect; it has no HP or damage to band.
- **How it plays at the table:** Cast 2 AP (HOUSE RULE, Tier 1 cost). Spells take effect immediately (sourcebook). Heals 2 HP on one ally or character in range; distance AP per the spell-range note is folded into the GM's ruling at the table.
