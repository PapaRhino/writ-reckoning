# Kess Varro — Forms (rival)

Conversion rule (sourcebook): Pokémon TCG numbers operate in the tens, so divide by 10 for WR play, consistently for every card. The converted HP and damage below govern at the table. Allies have HP and Damage but no set Defense — they use their controller's Command (4) as Defense (sourcebook). Allies vanish at 0 HP. Summon costs are HOUSE RULE: Tier 1 = 2 AP, Tier 2 = 3 AP, Tier 3 = 4 AP, Tier 4 = 5 AP, Tier 5 = 6 AP. Attacking with an ally costs 2 AP (HOUSE RULE). Kess has 6 AP per turn (HOUSE RULE).

## 1. Zoroark — Creature / Ally — Tier 3

- **Converted values:** HP 8, Damage 3
- **Summon cost:** 4 AP (HOUSE RULE)
- **Attack:** 2 AP, rolled with Command (sourcebook).
- **Notes:** A master of illusion. In a fight it is a genuine threat; out of a fight it is how Kess has already been in rooms she was never invited into. It fights to win for Kess, not to kill for her — if Kess retreats, it retreats with her.

## 2. Thief's Cloak — Equipment — Tier 1

- **Effect:** Defensive buff. Per the sourcebook's card-effect table, a defensive buff may be +1 DEF, damage reduction, or temporary HP — this cloak grants +1 DEF to its bearer while maintained (raising Kess's DEF from 4 to 5).
- **Summon cost:** 2 AP (HOUSE RULE); maintain a lasting effect costs 1 AP per turn (HOUSE RULE).
- **Notes:** Summoned directly onto Kess, it is immediately equipped (sourcebook). It persists only while she maintains it.

## 3. Smoke Bomb — Spell — Tier 1

- **Effect:** No damage. Fills the nearby area with thick smoke for 1 round.
- **Summon cost:** 2 AP (HOUSE RULE)
- **Notes:** HOUSE RULE: while the smoke holds, attacks through it require a Standard (5) Imagination check to find a target at all; a failed check spends the attack's AP with no hit. Kess uses it to disengage, not to win — a smoke bomb on the table means she is about to leave, and the party should decide quickly whether to let her.
