# Corvin Ash — Forms (villain)

Conversion rule (sourcebook): Pokémon TCG numbers operate in the tens, so divide by 10 for WR play, consistently for every card. The converted HP and damage below govern at the table. Allies have HP and Damage but no set Defense — they use their controller's Command (5) as Defense (sourcebook). Allies vanish at 0 HP. Summon costs are HOUSE RULE: Tier 1 = 2 AP, Tier 2 = 3 AP, Tier 3 = 4 AP, Tier 4 = 5 AP, Tier 5 = 6 AP. Attacking with an ally costs 2 AP (HOUSE RULE). Corvin has 6 AP per turn (HOUSE RULE).

## 1. Houndoom, Corrupted — Creature / Ally — Tier 3

- **Converted values:** HP 9, Damage 4
- **Summon cost:** 4 AP (HOUSE RULE)
- **Attack:** 2 AP, rolled with Command (sourcebook).
- **Notes:** The climax fight's front-line threat. Its flame is wrong — cold at the edges, ash-grey at the heart — and animals shy from it before it moves. It fights until dismissed or destroyed; it does not retreat.

## 2. Gengar Shade — Creature / Ally — Tier 2

- **Converted values:** HP 7, Damage 3
- **Summon cost:** 3 AP (HOUSE RULE)
- **Attack:** 2 AP, rolled with Command (sourcebook).
- **Notes:** A grinning shadow that slips along walls and strikes from behind cover. It cannot hold a door or carry weight — it is misdirection with teeth. If the party breaks the siphon (see the adventure), the shade weakens first: the GM reduces its damage to 2 for the rest of the fight (HOUSE RULE, siphon-linked).

## 3. Lodestone Siphon — Equipment (maintained) — Tier 3

- **Effect:** While Corvin maintains the siphon, his spell Forms deal +1 damage (offensive buff, per the sourcebook's card-effect table, fixed at +1 damage for clarity). Maintaining a lasting effect costs 1 AP per turn (HOUSE RULE).
- **Summon cost:** Already active when the climax fight begins — the party must break it, not wait for Corvin to summon it.
- **Breaking it:** A character adjacent to the lodestone rig may spend a simple interaction (1 AP, HOUSE RULE) plus a successful Standard (5) Craft or Imagination check to disrupt one siphon line; three disrupted lines break the siphon. See the adventure's climax notes for the full procedure and what happens when it breaks.

## 4. Frostbrand Spell — Spell — Tier 2

- **Effect:** 3 damage to a single target in range (2 base + 1 from the siphon while it runs; 2 damage once the siphon is broken).
- **Summon cost:** 3 AP (HOUSE RULE)
- **Notes:** A spell of stolen winter light. On a single-target critical success, double damage (sourcebook) — calculated from the damage actually dealt that turn, siphon bonus included.
