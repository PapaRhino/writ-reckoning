# Willa Bramble — Forms

Conversion rule (sourcebook): Pokémon TCG numbers operate in the tens, so divide by 10 for WR play, consistently for every card. The converted HP and damage below govern at the table. If your physical copy of a card shows different printed numbers, reconvert by dividing by 10 and use the reconverted numbers; keep the tier listed here unless the reconversion moves the card clearly into another tier, in which case the GM rules.

Willa's Card Pool is 4. Allies have HP and Damage but no set Defense — they use their controller's Command (3) as Defense (sourcebook). Allies vanish at 0 HP. Summon costs are HOUSE RULE: Tier 1 = 2 AP, Tier 2 = 3 AP, Tier 3 = 4 AP, Tier 4 = 5 AP, Tier 5 = 6 AP. Attacking with an ally costs 2 AP (HOUSE RULE).

## 1. Oddish — Creature / Ally — Tier 1

- **Converted values:** HP 5, Damage 1
- **Summon cost:** 2 AP (HOUSE RULE)
- **Attack:** 2 AP, rolled with Command (sourcebook).
- **Notes:** A small walking plant, excellent at finding water, sniffing out herbs, and slipping through undergrowth. In a fight it is a helper and a tripping hazard, not a weapon.

## 2. Bellsprout — Creature / Ally — Tier 1

- **Converted values:** HP 5, Damage 1
- **Summon cost:** 2 AP (HOUSE RULE)
- **Attack:** 2 AP, rolled with Command (sourcebook).
- **Notes:** Long reach for its size — it can pull a lever, snag a rope, or tug a small item from a ledge. Its grasp is the point; its damage is incidental.

## 3. Sunpetal Growth — Element / Nature — Tier 2

- **Effect:** A lasting nature effect. While maintained, one ally or player character of Willa's choice regains 1 HP at the start of their turn, as living growth knits small wounds.
- **Summon cost:** 3 AP (HOUSE RULE); maintain a lasting effect costs 1 AP per turn (HOUSE RULE).
- **Notes:** Essence cards are the most versatile Form type (sourcebook); this one is deliberately gentle — it steadies a fight, it does not win one alone. It cannot raise a character above Max Health.

## 4. Herb Poultice — Equipment (consumable) — Tier 1

- **Effect:** Restores 3 HP to one character when applied. Non-magical healing uses Craft (sourcebook's skill-check table); applying a prepared poultice needs no check unless the patient is in active danger, in which case the GM may call for one.
- **Summon cost:** 2 AP (HOUSE RULE); applying it is a simple interaction, 1 AP (HOUSE RULE).
- **Notes:** Once used, the poultice is gone — a summoned item that is used up is dismissed (sourcebook: a summoned item that would be destroyed simply disappears). Willa can summon a fresh poultice on a later turn by paying the summon cost again.
