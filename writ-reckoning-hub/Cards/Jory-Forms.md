# Jory Fenwick — Forms

Conversion rule (sourcebook): Pokémon TCG numbers operate in the tens, so divide by 10 for WR play, consistently for every card. The converted HP and damage below govern at the table. If your physical copy of a card shows different printed numbers, reconvert by dividing by 10 and use the reconverted numbers; keep the tier listed here unless the reconversion moves the card clearly into another tier, in which case the GM rules.

Jory's Card Pool is 4: Verdant's unstable energy deck (1 slot) plus 3 regular Forms. The unstable deck cannot be traded away without consequence (sourcebook). Allies have HP and Damage but no set Defense — they use their controller's Command (2) as Defense (sourcebook). Allies vanish at 0 HP. Summon costs are HOUSE RULE: Tier 1 = 2 AP, Tier 2 = 3 AP, Tier 3 = 4 AP, Tier 4 = 5 AP, Tier 5 = 6 AP. Attacking with an ally costs 2 AP (HOUSE RULE).

## 1. Verdant's Unstable Form — Basic Energy Deck — 1 card slot

- **Form:** A deck of basic Pokémon energy cards, the unstable form of Verdant, the Energy Shepherd (sourcebook).
- **Effect:** Per the sourcebook's card-effect table, "Draw a Card" means gain 1 AP. Once per turn, Jory may spend 1 AP to draw an energy from the deck and grant +1 AP — to himself or to one ally in near range — representing the right energy reaching the right Form at the right moment. The energy's type (grass, fire, water, and so on) is narrative flavor: it should match, where possible, the ally or spell it feeds.
- **Notes:** This deck is Jory's relationship with a Lesser Power made playable (sourcebook: unstable Forms require a special relationship with a Lesser Power). It cannot be lent, traded, or set aside lightly — the GM decides the consequence if a player tries, and it should be felt, not merely announced.

## 2. Eevee — Creature / Ally — Tier 2

- **Converted values:** HP 6, Damage 2
- **Summon cost:** 3 AP (HOUSE RULE)
- **Attack:** 2 AP, rolled with Command (sourcebook).
- **Notes:** Adaptable, quick, and fond of Jory in a way that makes the rest of the party slightly jealous. Its best use is as a scout's companion — it ranges ahead, doubles back, and warns before trouble arrives.

## 3. Trail Lantern — Equipment — Tier 1

- **Effect:** No combat damage. A shuttered lantern that burns steady in wind and rain, and whose light picks out fresh tracks, disturbed earth, and trip lines.
- **Summon cost:** 2 AP (HOUSE RULE); maintain a lasting effect costs 1 AP per turn (HOUSE RULE).
- **Notes:** Summoned directly onto Jory, it is immediately equipped (sourcebook). On tracking checks (Essence, per the sourcebook's skill-check table) made by lantern light, the GM may grant Clarity (d8 modifier die) — HOUSE RULE, used sparingly, only where the light genuinely helps.

## 4. Grass Energy — Element / Nature — Tier 1

- **Effect:** Offensive buff. Per the sourcebook's card-effect table, an offensive buff may be +1 to the attack roll or +1 to damage, at the player's choice — this energy is fixed at +1 damage for one ally's next attack, for clarity at the table.
- **Summon cost:** 2 AP (HOUSE RULE)
- **Notes:** The simplest expression of Verdant's gift: raw growth-energy, lent to a companion's blow. It is spent on that attack whether it hits or misses.
