# Rowan Kestrel — Forms

Character: `Characters/Rowan.md`. Card Pool 4 — 4 Forms listed, within pool.

## Conventions used here

- **Conversion (sourcebook):** Pokémon TCG numbers operate in the tens, so divide by 10 consistently for ALL cards (60 HP → 6 HP, 30 damage → 3 damage).
- **Tier bands as written (sourcebook):** Tier 1 usually ≤3 damage and ≤5 health; Tier 2 around 4–6 damage and <7 health; Tier 5 has no limits. Cards between Tier 2 and Tier 5 are placed by nearest band at GM discretion — each entry says how.
- **Allies (sourcebook):** allies have HP and Damage but no set Defense — they use their controller's Command (Rowan: 2) as Defense, and vanish at 0 HP.
- **AP costs (HOUSE RULE):** 6 AP per turn, refreshing at turn start. Summon Tier 1 = 2 AP, Tier 2 = 3 AP, Tier 3 = 4 AP, Tier 4 = 5 AP, Tier 5 = 6 AP; move near = 1 AP, far = 2 AP; attack with ally/item = 2 AP; simple interaction = 1 AP; maintain a lasting effect = 1 AP/turn.

## 1. Caterpie (Creature)

- **Card:** Caterpie (Pokémon TCG, classic print).
- **Type:** Creature/Ally.
- **Printed HP/damage:** 40 HP; String Shot 10 damage.
- **WR-converted values:** 4 HP, 1 damage (÷10).
- **Tier:** Tier 1 — damage 1 and health 4 are both within Tier 1's caps.
- **How it plays at the table:** Summon 2 AP (HOUSE RULE). A tiny scout and string-layer: it can lay trip-lines, mark trails, and squeeze into small spaces; attacks for 2 AP, defends with Rowan's Command (2), vanishes at 0 HP.

## 4. Fearow (Creature)

- **Card:** Fearow (Pokémon TCG, classic print).
- **Type:** Creature/Ally.
- **Printed HP/damage:** 70 HP; Drill Peck 40 damage.
- **WR-converted values:** 7 HP, 4 damage (÷10).
- **Tier:** Tier 3 (GM discretion) — health 7 reaches the Tier 2 <7 health cap and stops there, and damage 4 sits in Tier 2's range, but the combination (a 7-HP flying striker) plays a step above Tier 2, so it is placed one band up rather than squeezed into Tier 2.
- **How it plays at the table:** Summon 4 AP (HOUSE RULE). Rowan's one serious combat ally: a diving raptor that hits for 4 damage (2 AP per attack) and soaks 7 HP before vanishing — but it still defends with Rowan's Command (2), so it must strike and withdraw rather than stand and trade blows.
