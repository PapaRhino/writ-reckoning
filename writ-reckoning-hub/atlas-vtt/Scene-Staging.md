# Scene Staging — The Crater Call (prose notes, not a scene file)

**No Atlas scene file is included: the Atlas scene format is unverified, and
none is invented here.** Stage the scenes below by hand in Atlas from these
notes. See `README.md` in this folder for the tokens and their art.

## Map

- **Backdrop (available now, placeholder only):** the direction-test map in
  `Atlas-Test/pokemon-world-map-DIRECTION-TEST-not-final.jpg` — a color grade
  of Nick's composite Pokémon world map, explicitly labeled not-final. It shows
  Paldea on the west side with the Great Crater, which is enough to orient the
  table for session 1.
- **When it lands:** the Azgaar `.map` rebuild is a separate running task. When
  it arrives, it replaces the placeholder as the world map. Do not depend on it
  for tonight — the adventure plays from the notes alone.
- **Tactical maps (STUB):** no battle maps exist yet for Alderford, the
  Watcher's Shelf, or the Hollow Heart grotto. Run those scenes
  theater-of-the-mind, or sketch them on a blank Atlas scene. The adventure
  gives distances and positions in words (near/far, Center of Combat) so no
  grid is required.

## Token starting positions (by scene)

- **Opening + Scene 1 (Alderford):** party tokens (Alba, Tomas, Luz, Willa,
  Jory — whichever 4–5 are played) together at the hamlet center; Hedda with
  them; Tamsin at the storehouse; Kess entering from the west road.
- **Scene 2 (rim trail → Watcher's Shelf):** party + Hedda on the trail;
  crag hounds at the rust-red spring on the Shelf (no tokens made for the
  hounds — use generic beast tokens or theater-of-the-mind; their stats are
  inline in the adventure file).
- **Scene 3 (Watcher's Shelf):** party + Kess at the Shelf; Kess's position
  decides whether she ends as late ally or complication.
- **Scene 4 (Hollow Heart grotto, climax):** party entering the grotto;
  Corvin at the siphon with his corrupted allies; the siphon lines are the
  Center of Combat until broken line by line (see the adventure file).
- **Centers of Combat (from the adventure):** Scene 2 — the spring / the
  hounds; Scene 3 — Kess and what she learned; Scene 4 — the siphon, then
  Corvin. Turn order is by proximity to the Center (sourcebook), not
  initiative.
